Profiles

Create multiple profilesAs many as you say at once (up to 100), each with its own cookies and fingerprints: “Profile 3”, “Profile 4”… or the name you give, numbered. With “Collect in every new profile” on (Cookies, below), they all collect their cookies at once.
Replace a profile when closedClose a profile's window and the profile is deleted, with its cookies, website data, history and tabs, and a new one takes its place with the next number: “Profile 12” when the highest is “Profile 11”, then “Profile 13”… New fingerprints, no cookies (it collects new ones if “Collect in every new profile” is on). Not the first profile, which is never deleted.
Delete all profilesEvery profile except the first, with its cookies, website data, history and tabs.

Each profile opens in a window of its own, as big as the screen, with its own button in the taskbar (its name on its color), and has its own cookies, website data, history, tabs and fingerprints, so accounts stay apart. Open several at once. Bookmarks and settings are shared. The profiles are also in the 👤 menu and on the start page.

So that a site that checks finds a profile in daily use, not an empty one: Noor visits sites in a tab you don't see, in the profile's own cookies and with its own fingerprints and shields, while you use this profile or any other (switching doesn't stop it; many profiles collect at the same time, as many as “At once” says, the others waiting their turn). It reads each page a little, scrolls, accepts the cookie banner, and sometimes opens another page of the site, as a person would. Popular sites are the world's and those of the country of your time zone, in a new random mix each time. It never signs in or out, buys or downloads anything, and nothing goes into the history; your tabs stay as they are.

Extensions

Chrome Web StoreOpen an extension's page in the store, then press "Add to Noor" in the toolbar.
From a file or folderA .crx or .zip file, or a folder with a manifest.json.

Chrome web extensions run in every profile's normal tabs and can see and change the pages you open; the store's ones are kept up to date. Private tabs go without them, as Chrome's Incognito windows do. Extensions that block ads or change how pages look usually work; some Chrome-only features don't.

Shields

Block trackersThird-party files from Meta, Google Analytics, TikTok and other trackers never load.
Upgrade to HTTPShttp:// pages open over https://, and go back to http:// if the site doesn't support it.
Clean linksTracking codes such as fbclid, gclid and utm_* are removed from the addresses you open.

Shields are up on every site. Turn them off for one site from the 🛡 button in the address bar.

Fingerprint protection

Change all fingerprintsNew values for every protection, in this profile.

Websites see noisy canvas, WebGL and audio values and shifted ClientRects that differ from this PC's but stay the same on a site, another common PC's screen size, processor and memory, and Google Chrome on Windows. Sign-in sites of Meta, Google, Apple and Microsoft see this PC's real screen and hardware.
